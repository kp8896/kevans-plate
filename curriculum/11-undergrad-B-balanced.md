## Terms 1–3 — same as Plan A (~320 hrs)
Foundations → Core Major (Apple + AI-900) → Google Project Management.
See `10-undergrad-A-focused.md`.

## Term 4 — Data Literacy (~10 weeks, ~190 hrs)

| Course | Hrs | Cert | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Data Analytics** Professional Certificate | ~180 | ✅ | The brand-name data foundation: spreadsheets → SQL → visualization → analysis. Read and challenge any dashboard. |
| Kaggle Learn — Intro to Data / Pandas micro-courses | ~10 | ✅ free | Applied reinforcement; quick wins. |

**Milestone:** a data story — pull a dataset, analyze it, present 3 findings.

## Term 5 — Deeper AI / Technical Literacy (~8 weeks, ~80 hrs)

| Course | Hrs | Cert | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anthropic — Claude Code 101 + Intro to MCP + Intro to Agent Skills | ~5 | ✅ | See *how* AI agents/tools are wired — at altitude. |
| Anthropic — **Building with the Claude API** (flagship) | ~8 | ✅ | The strongest free Anthropic cert and the core of CCA-F prep. |
| **Stanford/DeepLearning.AI — Machine Learning Specialization** (audit for literacy) | ~50 | (audit) | Andrew Ng explains *what ML actually does*. Top brand. Pay for the cert only if you want it. |
| Anthropic — Prompt Engineering Interactive Tutorial (deepen) | ~10 | — | CCA-F covers prompt engineering heavily. |

**Milestone:** explain, in writing, what happens "under the hood" when an LLM
answers — tokens → context → tools — in plain language.

## Term 6 — Capstone Credential: CCA-F (~6 weeks, ~60 hrs)

| Course | Hrs | Cert | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| CCA-F structured prep (agentic architecture, Claude Code, MCP, tool design, prompt engineering) | ~40 | — | Pull together Term 5 into exam-readiness. |
| **Anthropic Claude Certified Architect – Foundations exam** | 2 | ✅ **proctored** | The capstone: a real, employer-recognized credential. Check partner-network eligibility for a free attempt. |
| (Elective) **Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst** *or* **Google AI Professional Certificate** | ~110 | ✅ | Pick one to broaden: ops analytics (Power BI, includes 50%-off PL-300) or applied AI. |

**Capstone deliverable:** design (on paper + a small working demo if you like) an
**AI assistant for your manufacturing workflow** — what it does, the tools/MCP it
would call, the prompts, and where a human stays in the loop.

## What you graduate with
A multi-disciplinary base — manufacturing, AI fluency, data analytics, project
management, technical literacy — capped by the **CCA-F proctored credential**.
This is a credible "bachelor's-equivalent" footprint across five megabrands.

finish A first and pause, just resume the clock — the ledger keeps your place.
