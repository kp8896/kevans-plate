Robotics + ML track.
**Best if:** you want the most complete, well-rounded foundation and don't mind a
~2-year horizon.

## Term 7 — "Understand the Code" Literacy (~7 weeks, ~95 hrs)

| Course | Hrs | Cert | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stanford — Code in Place** (intro Python, human-taught cohort) | ~45 | ✅ free | The single best "now I get what code is doing" experience — without committing to becoming a developer. Runs ~annually each spring; time it. |
| Kaggle Learn — Intro to Programming / Python | ~10 | ✅ free | Self-paced bridge if Code in Place isn't in session. |
| **DeepLearning.AI ML Specialization** — *complete for the certificate* (you audited it in Plan B) | ~40 | ✅ | Convert literacy into a marquee Stanford/Ng credential. |

**Milestone:** read a short ML script and annotate, line-by-block, what it's doing.

## Term 10 — Robotics/ML Bridge (~5 weeks, ~70 hrs)

| Course | Hrs | Cert | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MIT 6.S191** Intro to Deep Learning | ~15 | — | A fast, exciting survey that previews the graduate track. |
| **Modern Robotics — Course 1** (audit) | ~30 | (audit) | Taste the robotics spine before committing to the Master's. |

**Capstone deliverable:** a portfolio site/doc collecting your credentials, your
data project, your AI-assistant design (from Plan B), and a one-page "where I'm
headed in robotics + ML."

---

## What you graduate with
A broad, credentialed, genuinely bachelor's-shaped education — manufacturing, AI
(fluency → API → CCA-F), data (analytics → science), project management, real
programming literacy, liberal-arts breadth — and a running start into the
graduate Robotics + ML program.

## Honest note on scope
This is the most ambitious plan and the easiest to stall on. If momentum dips,
**fall back to Plan B and bank the degree**, then treat Terms 7–10 as electives
you pick off over time. The ledger makes that downgrade lossless.
