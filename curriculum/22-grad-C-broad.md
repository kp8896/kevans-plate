A substantial, portfolio-grade project integrating perception + RL + control +
(optionally) LLM task planning in simulation, with a written report at the level
you'd defend. This is your "thesis-lite."

---

## The accredited-degree bridge (optional)

Everything above grants **course/specialization certificates, not a degree.** The
one path to a *real, accredited Master's* in this list is:

> **Georgia Tech OMSCS** — *Computational Perception & Robotics* specialization.
> Online, ~$7,000–$10,000 total, accredited MS in Computer Science. Courses
> include Robotics: AI Techniques, ML for Robotics, computational perception.

**Recommended play:** use Grad Plans A–C as the *preparation that makes OMSCS
tractable* (admission + coursework), then enroll for the accredited credential.
Phases 1–11 map closely onto OMSCS prerequisites and early courses, so little is
wasted.

---

## What you finish with
Research-grade understanding across deep learning, RL (applied → theoretical),
computer vision, NLP/transformers, and robotics (Northwestern + ETH + UPenn) — a
defensible portfolio — and a clear, de-risked on-ramp to an **accredited MS** if
you want the formal degree.

## Honest note on scope
This is a multi-year commitment and includes free, ungraded, math-heavy elite
courses (CS285/CS229/CS224n) that are demanding. If the research depth stops
paying off for your goals, **bank Grad Plan B** and treat Phases 8–11 as optional
deep dives. The ledger keeps the downgrade lossless.
