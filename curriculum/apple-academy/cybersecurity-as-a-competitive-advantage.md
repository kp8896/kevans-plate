## Part 1 — CYBER THREATS

**Big idea:** threats are getting faster and cheaper to launch, and manufacturing
is a prime target. Understanding them is step one to defending.

- **2.2 What are cyber threats** — Common openings: **poor data handling, insecure
  devices, weak passwords.** **Social engineering / phishing** tricks people into
  handing over access. **Third-party / supply-chain** attacks ripple far beyond the
  first victim. **AI** makes attacks faster, cheaper, and more convincing. Impacts:
  data loss, downtime, financial loss, reputational damage, and onward spread.
- **2.3 What is ransomware** — Attacker gets in (phishing / weak credentials) →
  **encrypts or locks** key files → demands a **ransom (usually crypto).**
  Manufacturing is hit hard because **downtime is catastrophic** and supply chains
  are interconnected. **Ransomware-as-a-service** lets low-skill criminals rent the
  tools. The true cost (lost production, broken relationships) **dwarfs the ransom.**
- **2.4 Evolving threat landscape** — Time-to-compromise is collapsing:
  **2018 ≈ weeks → 2021 ≈ hours → 2025 ≈ minutes.** Digital transformation adds
  connected systems = a bigger **attack surface.** Everyone's connected, so everyone
  is affected — which is exactly why strong security is a business case, not a cost.

**Toby's one-liner:** *Phishing + weak creds get them in; ransomware monetizes
downtime; and they now move in minutes — so speed of defense matters.*

## Part 2 — CYBERSECURITY STRATEGIES

**Big idea:** security isn't just protection — done well it's a **competitive
advantage** (trust, contracts, uptime, lower costs).

- **3.2 Turning risk into a competitive edge** — Strong security **builds trust,
  wins contracts** (partners demand standards), **reduces downtime,** and **lowers
  insurance premiums & fines.** Avoid the traps ("too small to be a target,"
  one-and-done thinking, mis-investing). Steps: **know your valuable data/systems →
  make a plan → invest wisely.**
- **3.3 Understand your assets** — Inventory your **critical systems, data, and
  tech** (IT *and* the factory floor). Separate **truly critical** from merely
  important; **map who has access and how data flows;** keep the inventory current.
- **3.4 Updates add protection** — Patches **close known holes** attackers exploit;
  software maintenance is like equipment maintenance, and **speed matters.** Patch
  management = **(1) establish a process → (2) test, prioritize, deploy → (3)
  verify, monitor, maintain.**
- **3.5 MFA blocks account compromises** — Passwords alone aren't enough. **MFA**
  adds a second proof of identity, stopping attackers even with a stolen password.
  **Passkeys** are the newer, phishing-resistant, safer-than-passwords option.
- **3.6 Build trust through security** — Security, **user experience, and
  manageability** work together; security becomes a competitive advantage; **small,
  consistent actions** have outsized impact.

**Toby's one-liner:** *Know your assets → patch fast → turn on MFA/passkeys → and
sell the trust it earns you.*
