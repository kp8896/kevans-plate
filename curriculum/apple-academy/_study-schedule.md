## Why this order
- **SMT early** → you meet **AOI** before the courses that build on it.
- **Quality Fundamentals + QCO** → quality thinking + the data pipeline (image
  anomaly detection) before the ML course.
- **Automation** → robotics vocabulary (end effectors, metrology) before ML/robotics.
- **Preventive Maintenance** before **Predictive Maintenance (Data)** → strategy
  before the algorithms.
- **Machine Learning with Vision (#9)** sits *after* SMT, QCO, and Automation so its
  AOI/defect-detection use cases land on prepared ground — then **Predictive
  Maintenance (Data, #10)** closes the manufacturing→ML arc in Python.
