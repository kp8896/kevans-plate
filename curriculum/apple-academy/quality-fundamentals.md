## Part 1 — QUALITY FUNDAMENTALS

- **2.2–2.3 What is product quality:** **meeting customer needs and, where possible,
  exceeding them** (surprise & delight, attention to real-world use). Crucially —
  **quality is designed in from the start,** not just inspected/fixed later.
- **2.4 Why improve quality:** the **cost of quality** — the biggest costs hit
  *after* the sale (returns, warranty, reputation). Benefits accrue to **customers**
  (satisfaction, loyalty), **company** (lower total cost, less waste), **employees**
  (morale, pride).
- **2.6 Reliability testing:** the "quality circles" model — **product design →
  manufacturing variability → engineering performance → reliability.** Reliability
  testing types: **environmental, mechanical, real-use scenarios.**
- **2.7 Failure analysis toolbox** (memorize these five — they recur everywhere):
  **RCA** (root cause), **FMEA** (failure mode & effects → see PFMEA course),
  **Fishbone/Ishikawa**, **8D** (eight disciplines), **DMAIC** (Six Sigma cycle).
- **2.8 Organizational approach:** **TQM / QMS** (Quality Management System),
  universal quality principles, tailored per organization.

## Part 2 — SMART QUALITY (Quality 4.0)

- **3.2 The "iceberg" of quality:** what you see (visible defects) sits atop hidden
  causes (process, design, culture).
- **3.3 Quality evolution:** **1.0** = visual inspection (1800s) → **2.0** =
  standards + data/SPC (early-mid 1900s) → **3.0** = everyone's responsibility,
  continuous improvement, teamwork (late 1900s) → **4.0 = "smart quality" now**:
  digital tools, **real-time data, AI, automation** to *prevent* problems.
- **3.4–3.6 People + Process + Technology:** smart quality needs all three.
  **People** (judgment machines lack), **Process** (shift from reactive to
  predictive), **Technology** = the **four powers: Machine Learning, Data,
  Artificial Intelligence, Advanced Analytics.**

**Toby's one-liner:** *Quality is designed-in and customer-defined; it evolved
inspection → standards → culture → AI; and Quality 4.0 = People + Process + (ML +
Data + AI + Analytics).*
